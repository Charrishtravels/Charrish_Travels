Website, Brand Theme & Itinerary Document

A complete digital presence for Charrish Travels

Overview

This is where Charrish Travels' digital presence begins. Over three phases, we'll design a logo and brand theme, build a fully responsive website, and put together a digital itinerary document — so every touchpoint a traveller sees, from a Google search to a WhatsApp share, reads as one studio-quality brand from day one.

01 — Brand Theme
  • Logo Design
  • Primary & secondary colour palette (extended from the new logo)
  • Heading & body typeface pairing
  • Photography & iconography direction for destinations
  • Section motifs (dividers, markers) drawn from the new mark's line-art
  • Button, link & form styling rules
  • One-page style reference for future use
  • Applied consistently across web and the itinerary document
02 — Website

Pages

  • Home
  • Destinations / Packages listing
  • Package detail page
  • About Us
  • Gallery
  • Testimonials & Reviews
  • Contact
  • FAQ

Functionality

  • Customer review & rating submission form
  • New reviews held for approval before publishing
  • Every contact channel listed — phone, email, social, location
  • Responsive across mobile, tablet & desktop
  • Basic on-page SEO (titles, meta, sitemap)
03 — Itinerary Document
  • Layout built to the brand theme above
  • Featured destinations & services spread
  • Contact details
  • Digital PDF, optimised for WhatsApp/email sharing
Tools & Technologies
  • Design — Figma
  • Frontend — Astro
  • Hosting — Netlify / Vercel
  • Review & form handling — lightweight serverless backend
  • Itinerary Document — Figma, exported to PDF
